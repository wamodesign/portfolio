About Me

Two disciplines, one mindset

I live in the space where most designers never cross — structural packaging engineering and user experience design. With 11 years of experience, I have designed packaging across the full lifecycle: learning how materials behave, how dielines are designed and folded, and how a package survives the journey from factory to doorstep.

Along the way, I discovered that the box is only half the story. What truly matters is the person opening it — their anticipation, their confusion, their delight. That curiosity led me into UX: research, journeys, and interfaces designed around real human behavior.

Today, I work across the entire product experience — physical and digital, shelf and screen. Whether it's optimizing a dieline to reduce material costs or developing sustainable packaging solutions, my approach stays the same: understand the user, validate with data, and design with intention.

Signature Project — Where Both Worlds Meet

Modular NFC Smart Sticker Kit & Companion App

Bridging Physical Packaging & Digital Health for Elderly Medication Adherence

Lead UX & Packaging Designer — End-to-End Concept · Structural Thinking · UI Design  |  ArtiosCAD · Structural Engineering · WCAG Accessibility

The Problem

Elderly patients forget doses, take wrong amounts, or get lost in complex schedules — while tiny label text and standard packaging ignore age-related physical limitations. On the business side, high SKU diversity across bottles, blisters, and boxes makes a dedicated smart box per medication prohibitively expensive to manufacture.

The Pivot

Printing dynamic QR codes directly on artwork failed: per-SKU printing disrupts primary packaging lines, and QR scanning demands camera aiming elderly users struggle with. The solution — a modular NFC sticker that attaches to any existing packaging with zero production-line changes. Tap-to-Track replaces scanning: one tap logs the dose instantly.

Accessible by Design

The companion app follows WCAG 2.1: high-contrast colors, oversized typography, large touch targets, visual and audio reminders — plus a caregiver and doctor dashboard tracking adherence rates in simple reports.

✦ Cost-effective & scalable — works with all existing packaging, no machinery investment
